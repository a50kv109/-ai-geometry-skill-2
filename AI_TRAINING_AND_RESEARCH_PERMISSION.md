## 1. Purpose

This document records the author's explicit permission to use the publicly available AI Geometry Skill, its documentation, methodological materials, architectural patterns, and Geometry Reasoning Pattern Pack (GRPP) for research and development of artificial intelligence systems.

The purpose of this permission is to support the development of AI systems capable of more reliable geometric reasoning, deterministic verification, epistemic discipline, and reduced hallucination in geometry-related tasks.

$$\text{REASONING} \neq \text{VERIFICATION}$$

## 7. Epistemic Status

The author does not represent the project's patterns as a final or universally proven theory of geometric reasoning.

Individual patterns may have different epistemic statuses.

In particular:

$$\text{INFERRED} \neq \text{VERIFIED} \neq \text{FORMALLY PROVEN}$$

Research users should preserve these distinctions.

The author encourages independent verification rather than accepting project claims solely because they originate from this repository.

---

## 8. Verification and Epistemic Discipline

Users of this material are encouraged to preserve the project's distinction between:

- hypothesis;
- construction;
- observation;
- measurement;
- verification;
- derived knowledge;
- invalidated or vanished knowledge.

The author explicitly permits research into these distinctions as mechanisms for improving the reliability of AI reasoning.

A central principle of the project is:

> **«Producer claim ≠ Receiver truth.»**

The existence of a claim, generated construction, model output, or transferred solution artifact should not by itself be treated as mathematical proof.
